CONTEXTTRACE 60-CASE ANNOTATION — SIMPLE PROCESS

1. Give the complete pul folder only to Pul.
2. Give the complete sid folder only to Sid.
3. Tell each annotator: open START-HERE.md and follow it.
4. Wait until both complete packets are returned.
5. Give both returned packets to SAR.
6. SAR hashes and preserves both submissions before comparing them.
7. SAR computes field-level agreement but does not choose labels.
8. Pul and Sid then receive only the disagreement items.
9. Pul and Sid resolve those items together. Any item without agreement remains
   unresolved.

You do not need to run anything or inspect any annotation.
