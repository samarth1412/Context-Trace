## What V25 established

The benchmark selector sometimes drops QA answers while keeping questions:
32 of 400 AVeriTeC cases contain only question portions, including 11 inherited
supported and 8 inherited contradicted labels. Restoring only selected QA
records improves direct macro-F1 from 0.5213 to 0.5840 within the fixed V25
experiment. It does not automatically validate inherited labels.

The safe policy has one false support among 400 non-support examples, zero
contradiction false supports, 11% supported recall, 78% review recall, and a
49.4% review rate. These are development diagnostics, not confirmed product
metrics. The support-recall requirement remains 50%, review recall remains
80%, false-support rate remains at most 5%, contradiction false supports remain
zero, and review rate remains at most 50%.

The audit supersedes claims that V22–V24 demonstrated exhausted model features.
It also explains why another uninformed model sweep is not the next action.
Review label/evidence alignment first; rerun a preregistered development
comparison only once the data contract is sound. Relabeling never turns consumed
development examples into an untouched confirmation set.
